A collection of field notes from running eye-tracking studies on head-mounted
displays — what breaks, what the baselines look like, and how to report results
without fooling yourself.

## Calibration drift

Expect drift within 8–10 minutes of continuous tracking. Recalibrate between
task blocks, not just at the start of a session.

## Dwell-time baselines

Never compare dwell times across tasks with different target sizes. Normalize
against a per-participant calibration task first.
