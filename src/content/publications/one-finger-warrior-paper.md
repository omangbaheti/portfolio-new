**Status:** Accepted — IEEE ISMAR ’26 / TVCG

**Authors:** Omang Baheti, Shariff AM Faleel, Rishav Banerjee, Khalad Hasan, Pourang Irani

One Finger Warrior turns the index finger into a discreet, always-available input surface for smart glasses and other mobile devices. Thumb-to-finger interactions work through proprioception — one-handed, eyes-free, and usable without looking at the hand.

For the full write-up of the technique, the six-zone finger layout, the NSGA-III-optimized keyboard layout, and the treadmill evaluation, see the [One Finger Warrior project page](/projects/one-finger-warrior).

Full paper details and abstract will be posted here once the camera-ready version is out.
