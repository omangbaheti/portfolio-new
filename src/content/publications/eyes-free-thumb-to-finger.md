**Status:** Accepted — ACM VRST ’26

**Authors:** Rishav Banerjee, Shariff AM Faleel, Omang Baheti, Khalad Hasan, Pourang Irani

This work assesses the performance characteristics of eyes-free thumb-to-finger gestural text entry — how speed, accuracy, and learnability hold up when the hand is busy and the eyes are elsewhere.
